## Расхождения Шуберт vs Гёте
- 1. REFRAIN LINE 3 — 'ich finde' doubled. Schubert (all three refrain statements): 'ich finde, ich finde sie nimmer'. Goethe (Faust 1808, DTA p. 223): 'Ich finde ſie nimmer / Und nimmermehr.' Score evidence: first edition (Cappi und Diabelli, plate C. et D. No. 767), p. 3 underlay: 'Meine Ruh ist hin, mein Herz ist schwer, ich fin=de, ich fin=de sie nim=mer und nim=mermehr!'; likewise p. 5 (refrain 2: 'ich fin=de, ich fin=de sie nim=mer und nim=mer=mehr.') and p. 8 (refrain 3: 'ich fin=de, ich fin=de sie nim=mer und nim=mer=mehr.'). AGA (Breitkopf 1894, Serie XX No. 31), pp. 191, 192–193, 194–195: identical 'ich fin-de, ich fin-de sie'.
- 2. FINAL SECTION EXPANSION — Schubert's only word-level change plus threefold repeat. Goethe's poem ends (1808, one 8-line stanza): 'Mein Buſen draͤngt / Sich nach ihm hin, / Ach duͤrft’ ich faſſen / Und halten ihn! / Und kuͤſſen ihn / So wie ich wollt’, / An ſeinen Kuͤſſen / Vergehen ſollt’!' — sung once and finished. Schubert continues after 'Vergehen sollt'' with a repeat whose first line he rewrites: 'o könnt' ich ihn küssen, so wie ich wollt', an seinen Küssen vergehen sollt', an seinen Küssen vergehen sollt'.' The words 'o könnt' ich ihn küssen' have NO counterpart in Goethe (they replace 'Und küssen ihn' on the repeat); 'an seinen Küssen vergehen sollt'' is sung three times in all, 'so wie ich wollt'' twice. Score evidence: first edition p. 10: '…wie ich wollt', an sei===nen Küs=sen ver=ge==hen sollt', o könnt' ich ihn küs=sen so wie ich wollt', an sei===nen Küs==sen ver=ge====hen'; p. 11: 'sollt', an sei==nen Küs==sen ver=ge===hen sollt'.' AGA p. 196 identical ('sollt; o könnt' ich ihn küs-sen, so wie ich wollt; an sei-nen Küs-sen ver-ge-hen sollt; an sei-nen Küs-sen ver-ge-hen sollt.').
- 3. CODA — Schubert's structural addition. After Goethe's poem is over, Schubert brings back the first two refrain lines only: 'Meine Ruh' ist hin, / Mein Herz ist schwer.' (pp/ppp, decresc. e ritardando, ends with a period, no 'ich finde'). First edition p. 11: 'sollt'. Meine Ruh' ____ ist / hin, mein Herz ____ ist schwer.'; AGA p. 196: 'Mei-ne Ruh' ___ ist / hin, mein Herz ___ ist schwer.' In Goethe (DTA 1808, p. 225) the scene ends with 'Vergehen ſollt’!' and goes straight on to 'Marthens Garten.' — the fourth (partial) refrain return exists only in Schubert.
- 4. REFRAIN PLACEMENT ITSELF IS GOETHE'S, NOT SCHUBERT'S: the full refrain returns after 'zerstückt.' and after 'sein Kuss!' are in the poem. DTA 1808 TEI shows the scene as NINE stanza blocks with 'Meine Ruh’ iſt hin…' as blocks 1, 4 and 8 (the last block having eight lines); in the 1828 Ausgabe letzter Hand layout of ten 4-line stanzas these are stanzas 1, 4, 8 of 10. LiederNet/Rastl note (verbatim): 'Note: Goethe did not split the final eight lines into two stanzas prior to 1828.' Schubert sets all three Goethe refrains where Goethe put them and ADDS only the two-line coda (No. 3 above).
- 5. PUNCTUATION AS SUNG vs GOETHE 1808 (minutiae): (a) refrain 1 ends 'nimmermehr!' with exclamation mark in both scores (first edition p. 3, AGA p. 191); Goethe: 'Und nimmermehr.' — refrains 2 and 3 end with a period in the scores, as in Goethe. (b) 'Und ach, sein Kuss!' — comma after 'ach' in first edition p. 7 ('und ach, sein Kufs!'), AGA p. 194 and Rastl's critical text; Goethe 1808: 'Und ach ſein Kuß!' without comma. (c) The score runs stanzas together with commas where Goethe has full stops/exclamation marks: first edition p. 4 'ist mir vergällt, mein ar=mer Kopf…' (Goethe: 'vergällt.'), p. 9 'und hal==ten ihn, und küs==sen ihn' (Goethe: 'halten ihn!'). (d) First edition p. 6 has a stray engraver's comma 'Nach ihm nur, schau' ich' — not in Goethe, not in the AGA. (e) First edition p. 9 'ach dürft ich fas=sen' without apostrophe (= Goethe 1828 and Rastl's critical text 'Ach dürft ich fassen'); Goethe 1808 'Ach duͤrft’ ich' and AGA 'ach dürft' ich' have the apostrophe.
- 6. NO OTHER DIVERGENCES: apart from Nos. 1–3 the sung words are Goethe's unaltered. LiederNet's Schubert sung text (TextId 17756, SettingId 19751, research team Emily Ezust, Peter Rastl [Guest Editor], Johann Winkler) is headed verbatim: 'Gretchen am Spinnrade (Sung text for setting by F. Schubert) — Matches original text.' (LiederNet does not write out musical repeats, hence Nos. 1–3 are documented from the scores above.)
- 7. TITLE: Goethe's poem has no title. LiederNet/Rastl verbatim: 'Above the poem it is indicated "Gretchens Stube. Gretchen am Spinnrade allein."' The song title 'Gretchen am Spinnrade' comes from Schubert/the first edition (title page: 'Gretchen am Spinnrade aus Göthe's "Faust" in Musik gesetzt … von FRANZ SCHUBERT. 2tes Werk. … Wien, bey Cappi und Diabelli, am Graben No. 1133.', plate 'C. et D. No. 767', dedication 'Moritz Reichsgrafen von Fries').

## Повторы
- Goethe's own refrain scheme (must not be credited to Schubert): 'Meine Ruh' ist hin, / Mein Herz ist schwer, / Ich finde sie nimmer / Und nimmermehr.' opens the poem and returns after 'zerstückt.' and after 'sein Kuß!' — DTA Faust 1808 (pp. 223–225) shows exactly three occurrences (TEI blocks 1, 4, 8 of 9; = stanzas 1, 4, 8 of 10 in the 1828 layout). Schubert sets all three in place.
- Schubert repeat A ('ich finde, ich finde'): inside every refrain statement (not in the coda) — first edition pp. 3, 5, 8; AGA pp. 191, 192–193, 194–195.
- Schubert repeat B (climax return): refrain 3 enters after 'und ach, sein Kuss!' with a fermata — the spinning-wheel figuration stops and restarts (first edition p. 7–8: fermata chords after 'sein Kufs!', voice re-enters 'Meine Ruh' ist hin' pp; AGA p. 194 same, with accel./ff before the fermata). This dramatic stop is Schubert's, but the refrain words at this point are Goethe's stanza 8.
- Schubert repeat C (expanded ending): 'so wie ich wollt'' sung twice, 'an seinen Küssen vergehen sollt'' sung three times, with the newly worded link 'o könnt' ich ihn küssen' — first edition pp. 10–11, AGA p. 196 (fz on every bar of the repeats).
- Schubert repeat D (coda): fourth, partial refrain return — first two lines only, 'Meine Ruh' ist hin, / Mein Herz ist schwer.' — closing the song pp→ppp with a period, not an exclamation (first edition p. 11; AGA p. 196, 'decresc. e ritardando … dimin. ppp'). Goethe's poem ends at 'Vergehen sollt'!' — the framing return is Schubert's structural addition.
- No other word repeats exist anywhere in the setting: 'und ach, sein Kuss!' is sung once; stanzas 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 9 are sung straight through once (verified against the complete underlay of both scores, first edition pp. 3–11 = AGA pp. 191–196).

## Орфография
- ß → ss per 1996 reform (after short vowel): 'Zauberfluß' → 'Zauberfluss', 'Kuß' → 'Kuss'. Sources for the old forms: Goethe 1808 (DTA): 'Zauberfluß', 'Kuß'; first edition p. 7 engraves 'Zau=berflufs' and 'Kufs' (ſs ligature = ß); Rastl's Original Spelling (schubertsong.uk): 'Zauberfluß', 'Kuß'. 'fassen', 'Küssen' were already ſſ/ss (no ß involved), unchanged.
- Th → T: no words with Th occur in the sung text (rule applies only to metadata quotations like 'Göthe' → Goethe, '2tes Werk', 'componirt').
- Elisions/apostrophes kept AS SUNG per Rastl and the scores: Ruh', hab', schau', geh', Sein' (edle), wollt', sollt', könnt'. First-edition engraving is inconsistent (p. 3/5 'Ruh' without apostrophe vs p. 8/11 'Ruh''; p. 6 'sein' without apostrophe) — normalized to the forms of Goethe 1808, Rastl's critical text and the AGA ('Ruh'', 'Sein'').
- 'Ach dürft ich fassen' — NO apostrophe, following Rastl's critical sung text (schubertsong.uk Original Spelling: 'Ach dürft ich fassen') and the first edition p. 9 (high-res crop verified: 'ach dürft ___ ich fas=sen'), which agree with Goethe 1828. Variant: Goethe 1808 'duͤrft’' and AGA 'dürft'' print the apostrophe; either is defensible, we follow source hierarchy (first edition + Rastl).
- AGA idiosyncrasy: p. 193 prints 'sein' ed'-le Ge-stalt' with an apostrophe inside 'ed'le' (crop verified); we print 'Sein' edle Gestalt' per Goethe, Rastl and the first edition ('sein ed=le Ge=stalt').
- Punctuation policy: taken from the scores where they reflect the sung phrasing — refrain 1 ends 'nimmermehr!' (both scores), refrains 2–3 'nimmermehr.'; 'Mein Herz ist schwer,' with comma (Goethe 1808 + both scores; Rastl's semicolon 'schwer;' follows the 1810/1828 Goethe prints); 'Und ach, sein Kuss!' with comma (both scores + Rastl; 1808 has none); stanza 9 ends 'Und halten ihn,' with comma because Schubert runs on into 'Und küssen ihn' (Goethe: 'ihn!'); final coda ends with a period ('schwer.', both scores).
- Stanza layout: presented as ten 4-line Goethe stanzas (1828 layout) + Schubert's 6-line repeat block + 2-line coda. Rastl (verbatim): 'Note: Goethe did not split the final eight lines into two stanzas prior to 1828.' — in 1808/1810 the last eight lines are one stanza; DTA TEI confirms 9 blocks in 1808.

## Примечания
Definitive sung text established from the top of the source hierarchy: I read the COMPLETE first edition (Cappi und Diabelli, plate C. et D. No. 767, 11 pp., IMSLP #346755) and the COMPLETE AGA (Serie XX No. 31, pp. 191-196, IMSLP #13971) page by page, and collated them with Rastl's critical sung text (LiederNet SettingId 19751 and schubertsong.uk 'Original Spelling') and the Goethe Faust 1808 text from the Deutsches Textarchiv (plain text + TEI XML). Bottom line for the pilot: Schubert changes almost nothing in Goethe's words - the three big things a listener hears beyond the poem are (1) 'ich finde, ich finde sie nimmer' in each refrain, (2) the rewritten-and-repeated ending 'o koennt' ich ihn kuessen, so wie ich wollt', an seinen Kuessen vergehen sollt', an seinen Kuessen vergehen sollt'!', and (3) the two-line coda 'Meine Ruh' ist hin, mein Herz ist schwer.' The three FULL refrain returns are Goethe's own (stanzas 1, 4, 8 - verified in the 1808 TEI); only the coda return is Schubert's. The delivered stanzas array is the as-sung redaction in 1996 orthography (Zauberfluss, Kuss) with elisions per Rastl/score; the 6-line block (stanza 11) and the 2-line coda (stanza 12) are Schubert's additions and should be marked as such in the site's data model.
