# Певцы-шубертианцы: исследование для правил приоритизации

Статус: материал для расстановки приоритетов пользователем (2026-08-14). Ранги (canonical / major / notable) — предварительная оценка исследователей, НЕ утверждённые приоритеты. Утверждённые приоритеты — в docs/rules/youtube-performances.md.

## Мужчины: историческое и послевоенное поколение

- **Karl Erb** (tenor, 1877-1958, Germany) — _notable_
  Munich's legendary lyric tenor (creator of Pfitzner's Palestrina) who reinvented himself as a Lieder and oratorio singer; his late Schubert recordings, made in his 60s and 70s, preserve a spiritualized, head-voice-based style of tenor Lieder singing that influenced the post-war generation (Pears admired him). A key document of pre-war German Liedgesang.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: Schubert Lieder sides with Bruno Seidler-Winkler, HMV/Electrola, c. 1935-1951 (e.g. Der Einsame, Am See, Vor meiner Wiege, Der Juengling und der Tod; Electrola E 60676); Reissues: 'Karl Erb Liederalbum' (Preiser, CD)
  - YouTube: No official channel; fan uploads and 78rpm transfer channels plus Internet Archive (the Electrola 'Unvergaenglich, Unvergessen' series is on archive.org). Modest but findable amount of Schubert.
- **Richard Tauber** (tenor, 1891-1948, Austria (later UK)) — _notable_
  The most famous German-language tenor between the wars; recorded 12 of the 24 Winterreise songs for Odeon and dozens of individual Schubert Lieder, and popularized Schubert for mass audiences (Das Dreimaederlhaus, the 1934 film Blossom Time). His honeyed, portamento-rich Schubert is a style document of its era.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: Winterreise - 12 songs (some abridged), Odeon, 1927-28, with Mischa Spoliansky (reissued Jube 'Tauber Collection Vol. 14'); Numerous Schubert song sides, Odeon/Parlophone, 1919-1946 (reissues: Naxos Historical 8.110739 etc.)
  - YouTube: Very plentiful fan uploads and 78 transfers (Internet Archive has the Winterreise set); reissue-label Topic channels (Naxos Historical, Jube) carry his Schubert on autogenerated channels.
- **Hans Duhan** (baritone, 1890-1971, Austria) — _notable_
  Vienna State Opera baritone who made the FIRST complete recordings in history of both Winterreise and Die schoene Muellerin, for HMV in Vienna in the Schubert centenary year 1928 - a milestone every survey of Schubert on record starts from.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: Die schoene Muellerin, HMV Vienna, April 1928, with Ferdinand Foll (first complete recording); Winterreise, HMV Vienna, 1928, with Ferdinand Foll and Lene Orthmann (first complete recording)
  - YouTube: No official presence; complete Winterreise transfer is on Internet Archive (side-by-side uploads) and scattered fan uploads of Muellerin sides on YouTube.
- **Heinrich Schlusnus** (baritone, 1888-1952, Germany) — _major_
  The leading German Lieder baritone of the interwar era and Deutsche Grammophon/Polydor's house Lied singer; his hundreds of song sides set the pre-Fischer-Dieskau standard for bel-canto line and evenness in Schubert.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: Extensive Schubert song recordings, Polydor/DG, 1920s-1940s, with pianists Franz Rupp and Sebastian Peschko; DG LP compilation 'Heinrich Schlusnus singt Lieder von Schubert' (with Peschko); Preiser 'Lebendige Vergangenheit' reissues
  - YouTube: Large amount via fan/78-transfer channels; DG-catalogue reissues surface on autogenerated Topic channels. No complete cycles (he recorded songs, not full cycles).
- **Gerhard Huesch** (baritone, 1901-1984, Germany) — _canonical_
  Made the first great and essentially uncut recordings of Winterreise (1933) and Die schoene Muellerin (1934/35) with Hanns Udo Mueller for HMV - benchmark interpretations whose diction and legato directly influenced the Fischer-Dieskau generation. The historic reference point for both cycles.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: Winterreise, HMV Society issue, recorded 1-3 Aug 1933, with Hanns Udo Mueller (reissues: Pristine PACO005, Preiser, Naxos Historical); Die schoene Muellerin, HMV, recorded 1934/issued 1935, with Hanns Udo Mueller (Pristine PACO007)
  - YouTube: Both complete cycles circulate in multiple full-length fan uploads and 78-transfer channels; Internet Archive has individual sides; Pristine/Naxos reissues appear on Topic channels.
- **Herbert Janssen** (baritone, 1892-1965, Germany (later USA)) — _notable_
  Velvet-voiced Berlin and Met baritone whom Walter Legge used for his HMV song-society projects in the 1930s; his individual Schubert sides (Ganymed, Das Wirtshaus, Kriegers Ahnung, etc.) are treasured examples of interwar Lieder art, though he left no complete Schubert cycle.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: Schubert Lieder sides, HMV, 1930s (incl. Ganymed D544, Romanze from Rosamunde, Winterreise and Schwanengesang excerpts); Reissues: Marston 'Herbert Janssen: Portrait of a Mastersinger'; Pristine Classical Janssen collections
  - YouTube: Fan uploads and transfer channels only; a modest but choice amount of Schubert among mostly Wagner/Wolf material.
- **Alexander Kipnis** (bass, 1891-1978, Russian Empire/Ukraine (naturalized USA)) — _major_
  One of the very few true basses ranked with the greatest Lieder singers on record; his Schubert sides from the late 1920s-1940s (including Winterreise excerpts) combine enormous voice with unmatched interpretive refinement and remain reference points for low-voice Schubert.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: Winterreise excerpts (incl. Der Lindenbaum, Der Doppelgaenger), US Columbia, 1927-28, with Frank Bibb; Schubert Lieder with Gerald Moore, HMV, 1936; further Schubert sides for HMV/RCA Victor into the 1940s (?); Reissues: Nimbus Prima Voce, Marston
  - YouTube: No official channel; well represented through 78-transfer channels, Spotify/Topic autogenerated uploads of reissues, and Internet Archive.
- **Hans Hotter** (bass-baritone, 1909-2003, Germany) — _canonical_
  The great Wagner bass-baritone was equally a towering Schubertian: he recorded Winterreise at least three times, and his 1954 EMI version with Gerald Moore is a benchmark in the cycle's recorded history - the definitive dark, low-voice Winterreise for many collectors.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: Winterreise, DG, Nov 1942, with Michael Raucheisen; Winterreise, EMI/Columbia, 1954, with Gerald Moore (Great Recordings of the Century; Warner 2CD with Schwanengesang & Lieder, early 1950s); Winterreise, DG, 1961, with Erik Werba
  - YouTube: Plentiful: Warner/DG reissues on official and Topic channels, plus multiple complete fan uploads of the 1942 and 1954 Winterreise recordings.
- **Peter Anders** (tenor, 1908-1954, Germany) — _major_
  Germany's leading lyric tenor of the 1940s; his complete Winterreise and Schoene Muellerin taped for Berlin radio with Raucheisen in the final months of WWII are haunting historic documents and remain among the finest tenor versions of both cycles. Died young in a car crash.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: Winterreise, RRG Berlin radio, Jan-Mar 1945, with Michael Raucheisen (reissued DG 459 009, Tahra, Myto); Die schoene Muellerin, 1945, with Michael Raucheisen (radio; DG/Tahra reissues); second version 1948 with Guenther Weissenborn (various budget labels)
  - YouTube: Complete 1945 Winterreise available in full fan uploads and on Internet Archive; DG reissue tracks on autogenerated Topic channels.
- **Julius Patzak** (tenor, 1898-1974, Austria) — _major_
  Vienna's beloved stylist-tenor (the famous Das Lied von der Erde with Walter); despite modest vocal means his word-driven, deeply Viennese Schubert - both major cycles recorded - is prized by connoisseurs as an object lesson in diction and phrasing.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: Die schoene Muellerin, with Michael Raucheisen (German radio, 1940s; reissued Preiser); Winterreise, recorded Vienna (Palais Schoenburg), March 1964, with Joerg Demus (Preiser 2CD 93487; original issue Amadeo ?)
  - YouTube: Complete Winterreise available as full fan uploads; Preiser reissues on Spotify/Topic channels; scattered individual songs.
- **Aksel Schioetz** (tenor, 1906-1975, Denmark) — _canonical_
  His 1945 Die schoene Muellerin with Gerald Moore, made in London just after his heroic wartime years in occupied Denmark, is one of the classic recordings of the cycle - naturally boyish in timbre, plain-spoken and true. His career was cut short by a brain tumor in 1946.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: Die schoene Muellerin, HMV (DB 9115-22), autumn 1945, with Gerald Moore (reissued Seraphim 'Great Recordings of the Century', Danacord); Schubert song sides 1933-1946 (Danacord 'Complete Recordings')
  - YouTube: No official channel; the 1945 Muellerin circulates complete via fan uploads and Internet Archive 78 transfers; Danacord reissues on Topic channels.
- **Dietrich Fischer-Dieskau** (baritone, 1925-2012, Germany) — _canonical_
  The central figure of Schubert singing on record, full stop: he recorded virtually all ~400+ Schubert songs for male voice (DG, with Gerald Moore) and set interpretive standards for both cycles across seven-plus studio Winterreise versions over four decades. Any top-5 list for almost any Schubert song must reckon with him.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: Schubert: Saemtliche Lieder (complete songs for male voice), DG, recorded 1966-1972, with Gerald Moore (3-volume edition, 21 CDs); Winterreise: EMI 1955 and 1962 (Moore); DG 1971 (Moore); DG 1979 (Barenboim); Philips 1985 (Brendel); Sony 1990 (Perahia); plus 1948 RIAS (Klust, Audite); Die schoene Muellerin: EMI 1961 (Moore), DG 1971 (Moore); Schwanengesang: DG 1972 (Moore)
  - YouTube: Enormous presence: DG and Warner official/Topic channels carry the studio catalogue; many filmed recitals and complete-cycle fan uploads. The single best-covered Schubert singer on YouTube.
- **Hermann Prey** (baritone, 1929-1998, Germany) — _canonical_
  Fischer-Dieskau's great rival and temperamental opposite: warmer, more open-hearted Schubert, recorded across three labels and four decades. Founder of the Schubertiade Hohenems (1976), conceived around chronological performance of Schubert's output.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: EMI studio cycles, 1960s: Winterreise (with Karl Engel, c. 1961), Die schoene Muellerin (with Gerald Moore ?), Schwanengesang (with Wolfgang Sawallisch ?); Philips remakes of the cycles, early 1970s (with Karl Engel ?; Schwanengesang with Leonard Hokanson); Late live/studio cycle recordings, Denon and Capriccio, 1980s (with Helmut Deutsch, Philippe Bianconi ?)
  - YouTube: Well covered: Universal/Warner/Denon material on Topic channels, plus filmed recitals (including televised cycles) as fan uploads.
- **Fritz Wunderlich** (tenor, 1930-1966, Germany) — _canonical_
  His DG Die schoene Muellerin with Hubert Giesen, completed weeks before his fatal accident at 35, is probably the best-loved tenor recording of the cycle ever made - radiant tone and unforced directness that made him the tenor voice of Schubert for generations of listeners.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: Die schoene Muellerin + Schubert Lieder, DG, recorded Nov 1965 (lieder) and July 1966 (cycle), Munich, with Hubert Giesen; Various Schubert songs in recital recordings (DG/SWR archives; e.g. posthumous DG and hänssler live editions ?)
  - YouTube: Excellent: DG official/Topic channels carry the Muellerin (including a 'reference recording' full-album upload); vast fan-upload presence.
- **Ernst Haefliger** (tenor, 1919-2007, Switzerland) — _major_
  Distinguished Swiss lyric tenor (great Bach Evangelist) who recorded all three Schubert cycles for Claves with Joerg Ewald Daehler on an 1820s fortepiano - pioneering period-instrument Schubert cycles, sung with scrupulous musicianship into his 60s.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: Winterreise, Claves, recorded Sept 1980, with Joerg Ewald Daehler (Brodmann fortepiano c. 1820); Die schoene Muellerin, Claves, recorded June 1982, with Daehler; Schwanengesang, Claves, mid-1980s, with Daehler (?)
  - YouTube: Claves has an official channel and its catalogue is on autogenerated Topic channels; complete cycles streamable; moderate fan-upload presence.
- **Gerard Souzay** (baritone, 1918-2004, France) — _canonical_
  The great French melodie baritone was also one of the finest non-German Schubertians of the century: his Philips cycles with Dalton Baldwin bring an aristocratic legato and coloristic subtlety that made his Winterreise and Muellerin critics' favorites.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: Die schoene Muellerin, Philips, 1964, with Dalton Baldwin; Winterreise + 8 Lieder from Schwanengesang, Philips, recorded 1963 (2LP PHS 2-910), with Dalton Baldwin; Earlier Schubert recital LPs, Decca/Philips, 1950s (?)
  - YouTube: Universal-administered Topic channels carry the Philips recordings; many fan uploads; complete cycles findable but few official standalone videos.
- **Peter Schreier** (tenor, 1935-2019, Germany (DDR)) — _canonical_
  The leading German-speaking Lieder tenor of the later 20th century; his text-first, silvery Schubert is documented in multiple versions of all three cycles, crowned by the famous live Dresden Winterreise with Sviatoslav Richter. (Born 1935 - the boundary of this generation slice.)
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: Winterreise, Philips, live Dresden Semperoper, 17 Feb 1985, with Sviatoslav Richter (parallel Moscow tapes on Melodiya); Die schoene Muellerin and Schwanengesang, Eterna, early 1970s, with Walter Olbertz (?); All three cycles with Andras Schiff, Decca, late 1980s-early 1990s
  - YouTube: Strong: Decca/Philips/Berlin Classics material on Topic channels, the Richter Winterreise in complete uploads, plus filmed recitals.
- **Walter Berry** (bass-baritone, 1929-2000, Austria) — _notable_
  Great Viennese Mozart/Strauss bass-baritone and a respected recitalist, but verification shows his recorded Schubert legacy is thin: song selections from 1960s recitals and live programs (Demus, Werba, Peschko) rather than any widely circulated complete Schubert cycle. Include with caution - his Lieder fame rests more on Mahler and Brahms.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: Schubert Lieder selections in studio/recital recordings, 1960s, with Joerg Demus / Erik Werba (?); Live Moscow recital 1971 incl. Schubert group (?)
  - YouTube: Sparse for Schubert: scattered fan uploads (e.g. 'A Walter Berry Recital'); no official Schubert album presence found.
- **Theo Adam** (bass-baritone, 1926-2019, Germany (DDR)) — _notable_
  Dresden's leading Wagner bass-baritone was also an active Lieder singer in the DDR; his Eterna Winterreise and Schubert recital LPs represent the East German low-voice Schubert tradition of the 1970s-80s.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: Winterreise + Heine-Lieder from Schwanengesang, Eterna, c. 1975, with Rudolf Dunckel; Schubert Lieder recital LP, Eterna 825745 (?)
  - YouTube: Moderate: Berlin Classics (Eterna) reissues on Topic channels plus fan uploads; complete Winterreise findable.
- **Martti Talvela** (bass, 1935-1989, Finland) — _major_
  The giant Finnish bass championed Winterreise in recital throughout his later career; his BIS recording with Ralf Gothoni is one of the most admired true-bass versions of the cycle, extending the Kipnis/Hotter low-voice tradition. (Born 1935 - boundary of this slice.)
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: Winterreise, BIS (LP-253/254, CD-253), 1984, with Ralf Gothoni; Schubert song recordings on Finnish labels/recital tapes (?)
  - YouTube: BIS catalogue is on autogenerated Topic channels (Naxos distribution); complete Winterreise streamable; some fan uploads of live material.
- **Robert Holl** (bass-baritone, born 1947, Netherlands (based in Austria)) — _major_
  Dutch bass-baritone who made Schubert his life's work: fixture of the Schubertiade (Hohenems/Schwarzenberg) for decades, with an unusually deep repertoire of rare Schubert songs and ballads. NOTE: born 1947, he falls well outside this slice's pre-1935 generation - consider assigning him to the later-generation list.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: Winterreise + Heine-Lieder from Schwanengesang, with Konrad Richter (Preiser ?, 1980s); Schwanengesang, Hyperion, recorded Jan 2008, with Roger Vignoles; Winterreise, Challenge Classics, with Naum Grubert; numerous Schubert recital discs incl. rare songs (Brilliant/Globe Song Collection)
  - YouTube: Hyperion and Challenge catalogues on Topic channels since streaming licensing; limited official video; some Schubertiade recital fan uploads.
- **Kurt Equiluz** (tenor, 1929-2022, Austria) — _notable_
  Beloved Viennese concert tenor, immortal as Bach's Evangelist for Harnoncourt; his Schubert legacy is comparatively small but real - a late Winterreise recording and Viennese recital work. A connoisseur's inclusion rather than a core Schubertian.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: Winterreise, Christophorus (CD 74544), 1988, with Margit Fussi
  - YouTube: Sparse for Schubert: little official presence; occasional fan uploads. His YouTube footprint is overwhelmingly Bach.
- **Peter Pears** (tenor, 1910-1986, England) — _major_
  With Benjamin Britten at the piano he recorded both major cycles for Decca; their 1963 Winterreise - intensely inflected, with Britten's revelatory pianism - is routinely named among the greatest recordings of the work and defined English Schubert singing.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: Die schoene Muellerin + 3 Lieder, Decca, recorded 1958-59 (released 1960), with Benjamin Britten; Winterreise, Decca, recorded Oct 1963 Kingsway Hall (released 1965), with Benjamin Britten; Live Aldeburgh/BBC performances (Britten Pears Arts archive; 1955 festival Muellerin survives)
  - YouTube: Good: Decca/Universal Topic channels carry both cycles complete; additional fan uploads and BBC material.
- **Anton Dermota** (tenor, 1910-1989, Slovenia/Austria) — _notable_
  Slovene-born Vienna State Opera lyric tenor (legendary post-war Mozart ensemble) who was also a serious Lieder recitalist; recorded both major Schubert cycles with his wife Hilda Dermota at the piano - elegant, Viennese-schooled tenor Schubert.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: Die schoene Muellerin, with Hilda Dermota (piano); Preiser LP reissue 1977 (original recording 1950s-60s ?); Winterreise, 1962, with Hilda Dermota (?)
  - YouTube: Fan uploads only (individual songs such as Fruehlingstraum circulate); Preiser reissues partially on streaming Topic channels.
- **Josef Greindl** (bass, 1912-1993, Germany) — _notable_
  Bayreuth's iron-voiced post-war bass recorded a complete Winterreise for DG in 1957 - one of the few true-bass studio versions of its decade and a document of the German low-voice Lieder tradition between Kipnis and Talvela.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: Winterreise, DG, 1957, with Hertha Klust (mono; reissued digitally by DG)
  - YouTube: Complete Winterreise available as fan uploads; DG digital reissue on streaming/Topic channels. Otherwise limited Schubert.

## Мужчины: современные поколения

- **Thomas Quasthoff** (bass-baritone, 1959-, Germany) — _canonical_
  One of the most beloved Lieder singers of the post-Fischer-Dieskau era; his warm, huge-ranged bass-baritone and direct communication made his Schubert cycles benchmarks of the 1990s-2000s. Won the 2004 Grammy (Best Classical Vocal Performance) for the Schubert orchestral-Lieder disc with Abbado. Retired from classical singing in January 2012, which closes his discography.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: Goethe-Lieder — Charles Spencer, piano (RCA Red Seal, rec. May 1993, rel. 1995; cat. 09026-61864-2); Winterreise — Charles Spencer (RCA Red Seal, rec. Feb 1998, rel. 1998); Schwanengesang D 957 + Brahms Vier ernste Gesänge — Justus Zeyen (Deutsche Grammophon, rel. Mar 2001; Grammy-nominated); Schubert: Lieder with Orchestra — with Anne Sofie von Otter, Chamber Orchestra of Europe / Claudio Abbado (DG, rel. Apr 2003; won Grammy 2004; reissued 2013); Die schöne Müllerin — Justus Zeyen (DG, rel. 4 Oct 2005); Winterreise — Daniel Barenboim, DVD filmed at the Berlin Philharmonie (DG DVD, rel. Oct 2005; reissued as digital 'Visual Album' Feb 2021); Possibly scattered Schubert items on DG recital compilations (e.g. A Romantic Songbook, 2004)?
  - YouTube: Very good: DG and Sony/RCA auto-generated Topic channels carry all six albums as full official audio; excerpts of the Barenboim Winterreise film on DG/Unitel channels; abundant fan uploads of live performances (Verbier, Salzburg).
- **Matthias Goerne** (baritone, 1967-, Germany) — _canonical_
  Arguably the most-recorded Schubert baritone of his generation; pupil of Fischer-Dieskau and Schwarzkopf. His 12-volume Matthias Goerne Schubert Edition on harmonia mundi (2008-2014, boxed 2016) is the largest single-singer Schubert survey of recent decades, and he has recorded Winterreise at least three times.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: Winterreise — Graham Johnson (Hyperion Schubert Edition Vol. 30, 1997); Winterreise — Alfred Brendel, live Wigmore Hall (Decca, 2004); Die schöne Müllerin — Eric Schneider (Decca, c. 2001?); Matthias Goerne Schubert Edition, 12 vols incl. all three cycles with Christoph Eschenbach, plus pianists Leonskaja, Haefliger, Deutsch, Schneider et al. (harmonia mundi, 2008-2014; 12-CD box 2016)
  - YouTube: Excellent: harmonia mundi's official channel and Topic channels stream the whole HM edition; Decca/Universal Topic audio; Hyperion available since 2023; many full live cycles as fan uploads and festival streams.
- **Christian Gerhaher** (baritone, 1969-, Germany) — _canonical_
  Widely regarded as today's supreme male Schubert interpreter for text-focused, quasi-spoken delivery; lifelong duo with pianist Gerold Huber. His Winterreise won an Echo Klassik in 2002 and his Schubert recital discs are modern references.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: Early cycle set incl. Winterreise (rec. Jan 2001), Die schöne Müllerin, Schwanengesang, Harfner-Gesänge — Gerold Huber (Arte Nova/Sony budget label, c. 1999-2003; Echo Klassik 2002 for Winterreise); Die schöne Müllerin — Huber (RCA Red Seal, 2003); Abendbilder, Schubert recital — Huber (RCA Red Seal, 2006?); Nachtviolen, Schubert recital — Huber (Sony Classical, 2014); Winterreise, new studio recording — Huber (Sony Classical, 2022?)
  - YouTube: Good: Sony Classical Topic channels carry the RCA/Sony albums; occasional official videos and broadcast recitals (BR-Klassik); Arte Nova items patchier; fan uploads of live Wigmore/Schubertiade performances.
- **Ian Bostridge** (tenor, 1964-, United Kingdom) — _canonical_
  The defining English Schubert tenor of the last 30 years, with an intensely dramatic, intellectual approach; has recorded Winterreise at least three times and wrote the acclaimed book 'Schubert's Winter Journey: Anatomy of an Obsession' (2015).
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: Die schöne Müllerin — Graham Johnson, with Fischer-Dieskau reciting the Müller prologue/epilogue (Hyperion Schubert Edition Vol. 25, 1996); Winterreise — Julius Drake, film directed by David Alden (NVC Arts/Warner DVD, 1997); Winterreise — Leif Ove Andsnes (EMI, rel. 2004); Die schöne Müllerin — Mitsuko Uchida (EMI, 2005); Schwanengesang — Antonio Pappano (EMI, 2009); Winterreise — Thomas Adès, live (Pentatone, 2019); Schwanengesang — Lars Vogt (rel. Sep 2022, Pentatone?); EMI Schubert recital(s) with Julius Drake, late 1990s?
  - YouTube: Excellent: Warner Classics and Pentatone Topic channels carry the audio; clips of the 1997 Alden Winterreise film circulate widely (largely fan uploads); many interviews/lectures on Schubert.
- **Mark Padmore** (tenor, 1961-, United Kingdom) — _canonical_
  His Schubert trilogy with Paul Lewis is one of the most acclaimed of the century — the Winterreise won the 2010 Gramophone Vocal Award. A plain-spoken, Evangelist-like directness makes his cycles a standard modern recommendation for tenor voice.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: Winterreise — Paul Lewis (harmonia mundi, 2009; Gramophone Vocal Award 2010); Die schöne Müllerin — Paul Lewis (harmonia mundi, 2010); Schwanengesang — Paul Lewis (harmonia mundi, 2011); Winterreise — Kristian Bezuidenhout, fortepiano (harmonia mundi, 2017?); Schwanengesang + Beethoven An die ferne Geliebte — Mitsuko Uchida (Decca, rec. announced 2021, rel. 2022?)
  - YouTube: Good: harmonia mundi and Decca Topic channels have full official audio; Wigmore Hall channel and festival streams carry live Padmore Schubert recitals; modest official video otherwise.
- **Christoph Prégardien** (tenor, 1956-, Germany) — _canonical_
  A central Schubert tenor since the early 1990s, prized for style, diction and his pioneering fortepiano collaborations with Andreas Staier; his Challenge Classics Die schöne Müllerin with Michael Gees won MIDEM Record of the Year 2009.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: Die schöne Müllerin — Andreas Staier, fortepiano (Deutsche harmonia mundi, early 1990s?); Winterreise — Andreas Staier, fortepiano (Teldec, 1997?); Die schöne Müllerin — Michael Gees (Challenge Classics, 2008; MIDEM Record of the Year 2009); Schwanengesang — Andreas Staier (Challenge Classics, 2008); Winterreise — Michael Gees (Challenge Classics, 2013; also DVD); 3-CD box of the three cycles (Challenge Classics CC72665)
  - YouTube: Good: Challenge Records has an official channel with videos/trailers; Warner (Teldec) and Sony (DHM) Topic channels cover the 1990s recordings; fan uploads plentiful.
- **Peter Schreier** (tenor, 1935-2019, Germany) — _canonical_
  Boundary case for this slice (born 1935, career to 2005): the leading German Schubert tenor of the late 20th century. His live Winterreise with Sviatoslav Richter and his Decca cycles with András Schiff are classics; include or hand to the older-generation slice as the project prefers.

## Женщины: историческое и послевоенное поколение

- **Elena Gerhardt** (mezzo, 1883-1961, Germany (Leipzig; emigrated to London 1934)) — _major_
  The pioneering female Lieder recitalist — essentially the first singer to build an entire career on Lieder rather than opera, and the first to make Schubert song recording an art form. Her 1907 G&T and 1911 HMV discs with conductor Arthur Nikisch at the piano (An die Musik, Wohin?, Du bist die Ruh) are foundational documents of Schubert singing.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: G&T Berlin 78s with Arthur Nikisch, piano (1907); HMV Red Label series with Nikisch (1911) — incl. An die Musik, Wohin?, Du bist die Ruh; Schubert centennial-era HMV 78s (1926-28) — Litanei, Fischerweise, Das Fischermaedchen; Reissues: 'Elena Gerhardt — A Centenary Tribute' and 78rpm-transfer anthologies
  - YouTube: No official channel presence; decent number of fan/archive 78rpm-transfer uploads (e.g. the 1907/1911 Nikisch sides, An die Musik). Quality varies; quantity moderate.
- **Julia Culp** (mezzo, 1880-1970, Netherlands) — _notable_
  'The Dutch nightingale', an internationally celebrated concert/Lieder singer 1901-1919 with famously smooth, intimate delivery. Made ~90 recordings 1906-1926 (Anker, Odeon, HMV, Victor, Electrola) including Schubert (Der Tod und das Maedchen, Heidenroeslein) — an important pre-electric witness to Schubert style.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: Victor acoustic 78s, New York (1914-17) — incl. Heidenroeslein (1914); Gramophone/Electrola sides (to 1926) — incl. Der Tod und das Maedchen; Reissue: 'Julia Culp: The Complete Victor and Electrola Recordings 1914-26' (Marston, 2-CD)
  - YouTube: Fan uploads and Internet-Archive-sourced transfers only; small quantity of Schubert (Heidenroeslein 1914 is findable).
- **Elisabeth Schumann** (soprano, 1888-1952, Germany (later UK/USA)) — _canonical_
  For many collectors the classic pre-war female Schubert voice — silvery, poised lyric soprano; Lotte Lehmann said she represented 'perhaps the purest singing style of German Lieder'. Recorded ~28 Schubert songs for HMV between 1927 and 1949 (pianists George Reeves, Gerald Moore, Karl Alwin), long the reference versions of the lighter lyric Schubert songs.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: HMV Schubert 78s (1927-1945/49), with Reeves/Moore/Alwin — collected on Naxos Historical 8.110731 'Schubert Lieder (1927-1945)' (28 songs); 78 of Gretchen am Spinnrade with Gerald Moore (HMV — transfer on Internet Archive)
  - YouTube: Plentiful: Naxos Historical material via Topic-type channels plus many fan 78-transfer uploads; most of her core Schubert is findable.
- **Lotte Lehmann** (soprano, 1888-1976, Germany (naturalized USA)) — _canonical_
  Great singing-actress who became the first woman to record Schubert's complete Winterreise (RCA/Columbia, March 1941, with Paul Ulanowsky), plus dozens of other Schubert songs 1927-1949. Her word-driven, emotionally direct Schubert shaped a whole American generation via her Town Hall recitals and teaching (students incl. Grace Bumbry, Marilyn Horne).
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: Winterreise, with Paul Ulanowsky (RCA Victor/Columbia 78 albums, rec. 4 & 14 March 1941) — first female recording; reissued Pearl/Naxos; Gretchen am Spinnrade D 118 (ca. 1941, RCA-era sessions); Odeon/Victor Schubert 78s from 1927 onward (Ave Maria, Staendchen, An die Musik etc.)
  - YouTube: Abundant fan uploads plus Lotte Lehmann League materials; the 1941 Winterreise circulates complete. No major official-label channel but coverage is broad.
- **Kirsten Flagstad** (soprano, 1895-1962, Norway) — _notable_
  The century's great Wagner soprano; her Schubert legacy is real but secondary — a Decca Schubert/Schumann Lieder recital and several Schubert songs among her RCA song recitals, sung with monumental calm rather than idiomatic Lieder intimacy. Include as a famous voice visiting Schubert rather than a core Schubertian.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: RCA Victor song recitals with Edwin McArthur, piano (1950-52) — include 4 Schubert songs (Pristine Audio reissue PACO079 series); 'Lieder Recital: Schubert & Schumann' with Edwin McArthur (Decca LXT 5263 / London LL 1546, mid-1950s)
  - YouTube: Decca/Universal Topic channels carry some of her recital material; rest is fan uploads. Schubert quantity modest.
- **Marian Anderson** (contralto, 1897-1993, USA) — _major_
  Schubert was central to her concert identity: Toscanini's famous 'a voice heard once in a hundred years' remark followed her Schubert singing at Salzburg (1935), and 'Ave Maria' D 839 was a lifelong signature (sung at the 1939 Lincoln Memorial concert). Her dramatic Erlkoenig and Der Tod und das Maedchen are classics of contralto Schubert.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: Ave Maria D 839 (RCA Victor, 1946 remake; earlier HMV/Victor versions from the 1930s with Kosti Vehanen); Der Tod und das Maedchen D 531 (RCA, 1946, with Franz Rupp); Erlkoenig D 328 (RCA, 1951); Complete edition: 'Beyond the Music — Her Complete RCA Victor Recordings' (Sony, 15 CDs, 2021)
  - YouTube: Strong: Sony official/Topic uploads plus very many fan uploads; Ave Maria versions have millions of views. Erlkoenig and Der Tod und das Maedchen easily found.
- **Ria Ginster** (soprano, 1898-1985, Germany (later Zurich, Switzerland)) — _notable_
  A connoisseur's concert soprano of the 1930s, admired by accompanists and collectors; made refined Schubert 78s for HMV with Gerald Moore (Wiegenlied D 867, 1933; Auflloesung; Liebesbotschaft) and appeared in the HMV song-society projects of the decade. Small but distinguished recorded Schubert legacy.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: HMV Schubert 78s with Gerald Moore (1933-35) — Wiegenlied D 867, Aufloesung, Liebesbotschaft; Reissue: 'The Art of Ria Ginster' (Pearl/other historic labels)
  - YouTube: Sparse — a handful of fan transfers and IDAGIO-adjacent uploads; the weakest YouTube coverage on this list.
- **Elisabeth Gruemmer** (soprano, 1911-1986, Germany (born Niederjeutz, Alsace-Lorraine)) — _notable_
  One of the purest German lyric sopranos of the post-war era; her 1958 Electrola LP of Schubert and Brahms Lieder with Gerald Moore is a collector's touchstone for unaffected, silver-toned Schubert singing, though her Lieder discography is compact.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: 'Elisabeth Gruemmer singt Lieder von Schubert und Brahms' with Gerald Moore (Electrola/HMV, 1958; CD reissue 1996)
  - YouTube: Moderate: fan uploads of the 1958 LP tracks; some Warner-controlled material on Topic channels. Not large but the key items are up.
- **Kathleen Ferrier** (contralto, 1912-1953, England) — _canonical_
  Britain's beloved contralto whose few Schubert recordings (Decca, 1947-52) acquired iconic status after her early death — An die Musik, Der Musensohn, Du bist die Ruh, Die junge Nonne. The live 1949 Edinburgh Festival recital with Bruno Walter at the piano (Schubert/Schumann/Brahms) is one of the most famous Lieder documents ever broadcast.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: Schubert 78s with Phyllis Spurr, piano (Decca, 1947) — incl. Gretchen am Spinnrade D 118 (rec. 14 March 1947) and Die junge Nonne; The Legendary Edinburgh Festival Recital with Bruno Walter, piano (BBC broadcast 1949; issued by Decca); Schubert/Brahms studio sides with Walter (Decca, 1949)
  - YouTube: Excellent: Decca/UMG Topic channels plus vast fan uploads; the Edinburgh recital and studio Schubert are all findable.
- **Elisabeth Schwarzkopf** (soprano, 1915-2006, Germany (naturalized British)) — _canonical_
  With Wolf, Schubert was one of the two cornerstones of Schwarzkopf's entire recital career; her October 1952 Abbey Road recital with pianist Edwin Fischer is routinely cited as one of the greatest Schubert records ever made, and she kept re-recording the core repertoire with Gerald Moore and Geoffrey Parsons into the 1970s. THE project priority: her 1952 Gretchen am Spinnrade with Fischer is a benchmark for the pilot song.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: Schubert Lieder recital with Edwin Fischer (Columbia/EMI, rec. Abbey Road 4-7 Oct 1952, issued 1953) — 12 songs: An die Musik, Im Fruehling, Wehmut, Ganymed, Das Lied im Gruenen, Gretchen am Spinnrade, Naehe des Geliebten, Die junge Nonne, An Sylvia, Auf dem Wasser zu singen, Nachtviolen, Der Musensohn; Early Columbia 78s with Gerald Moore (1948-1954) — incl. Am Tage Aller Seelen, Ungeduld (collected on Naxos Historical 8.111287 'Lieder 1952-1954'); 'A Lieder Recital' and 'The Elisabeth Schwarzkopf Songbook' series with Gerald Moore (Columbia/EMI, mid-1950s-1960s) — Schubert groups throughout; Live Salzburg Festival recitals with Gerald Moore (from 19 Aug 1957) — Orfeo 3-CD 'Schwarzkopf sings Schubert & Wolf' (C826103) and Orfeo 'Liederabende Salzburger Festspiele' volumes; 'Homage to Gerald Moore', live Royal Festival Hall farewell concert (EMI, Feb 1967) — includes Schubert; 'Songs I Love' with Geoffrey Parsons (EMI, rec. 1970-73) — late Schubert remakes; EMI 'Great Recordings of the Century: Schubert — 24 Lieder' combines the Fischer 1952 recital with Moore/Parsons items; Warner box 'The Complete Recitals 1952-1974' contains all of the above studio material
  - YouTube: Very strong: Warner Classics official and Topic channels (1952 Fischer recital complete), Orfeo label uploads of the Salzburg lives, plus huge numbers of fan uploads. Best-covered female Schubertian on YouTube along with Ferrier and Ameling.
- **Hilde Gueden** (soprano, 1917-1988, Austria) — _notable_
  Star Viennese Mozart/Strauss soprano of the Decca stable, noted generally for Lieder and oratorio, but verification found NO significant solo Schubert Lieder album — her famous Gulda-accompanied Decca song recital (1956) is Richard Strauss, not Schubert. Suggest treating her as marginal for this project unless individual Schubert items surface.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: No significant verified solo Schubert Lieder recording found; possible scattered individual songs on Decca recital/operetta discs (?)

- **Irmgard Seefried** (soprano, 1919-1988, Germany (Bavaria; career in Vienna)) — _major_
  Beloved Vienna ensemble soprano and natural, spontaneous recitalist; recorded substantial Schubert for DG with her regular pianist Erik Werba in the 1950s-60s, including a 1962 stereo Gretchen am Spinnrade. Her Schubert is prized for artless charm and Viennese warmth.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: Schubert Lieder with Erik Werba (DG, mono/stereo sessions mid-1950s-1962; stereo Schubert recital 1962 incl. Gretchen am Spinnrade) — reissued as 'The Art of Irmgard Seefried Vol. 4: Schubert Lieder' (Decca Eloquence); Lieder von Schubert, Schumann, Brahms LP (DG, 1950s); Live Salzburg recitals with Werba (Orfeo)
  - YouTube: Good: DG/Eloquence Topic channels plus fan uploads; the Werba Schubert recital is streamable.
- **Lisa della Casa** (soprano, 1919-2012, Switzerland) — _notable_
  The great Arabella/Strauss soprano left one rare but treasurable Decca Lieder recital whose Schubert group (incl. Gretchen am Spinnrade) shows her pure, unsentimental pre-war-style Lieder manner. A small legacy, but its Gretchen is directly relevant to the pilot song.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: Lieder Recital with Karl Hudez, piano (Decca, late 1950s) — Schubert group: Auf dem Wasser zu singen D 774, Im Fruehling D 882, Du bist die Ruh D 776, Gretchen am Spinnrade D 118; reissued as 'Lisa Della Casa in Recital' (Decca Eloquence)
  - YouTube: Modest: Eloquence/Decca Topic uploads of the recital plus scattered fan uploads.
- **Rita Streich** (soprano, 1920-1987, Germany (born Barnaul, Russia)) — _notable_
  Famed coloratura ('the German nightingale') who was also a dedicated Lieder singer; recorded a full DG Schubert recital in 1960 with Erik Werba, including Der Hirt auf dem Felsen with clarinettist Heinrich Geuser — a classic of light-voiced Schubert singing.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: 'Rita Streich singt Lieder von Franz Schubert' with Erik Werba, piano, and Heinrich Geuser, clarinet (DG, 1960); 'Liederabend / A Song Recital' with Werba (DG) — includes Schubert group (e.g. Die Voegel D 691)
  - YouTube: Good: DG Topic channels carry the 1960 recital; fan uploads add more. Gretchen am Spinnrade: no recording traced (?).
- **Sena Jurinac** (soprano, 1921-2011, Bosnia (Travnik) / Austria) — _notable_
  Adored Vienna Mozart-ensemble soprano, but verification found essentially no Schubert Lieder discography — her celebrated song recordings are Schumann (Frauenliebe und -leben, Westminster/DG) and Strauss (Four Last Songs, 1951). Recommend excluding her from the Schubert singer list or listing only as a marginal figure.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: No significant verified Schubert Lieder recording found (her recital fame on disc rests on Schumann and R. Strauss); possible isolated live Schubert items (?)
  - YouTube: Much Jurinac on YouTube (opera, Schumann, Strauss) but virtually no Schubert Lieder.
- **Victoria de los Angeles** (soprano, 1923-2005, Spain) — _notable_
  One of the best-loved recitalists of the LP era; Schubert formed a regular part of her EMI recital programs with Gerald Moore (studio recital LPs and the famous live gala evenings with Schwarzkopf and Fischer-Dieskau), though her core fame rests on Spanish and French repertoire. A warm, personal Schubert stylist rather than a specialist.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: Schubert groups on EMI recital LPs with Gerald Moore (1950s-60s, e.g. 'Songs by Schubert, Brahms, Faure...' with Moore and Gonzalo Soriano); 'An Evening with de los Angeles, Schwarzkopf, Fischer-Dieskau and Gerald Moore' (EMI, live 1967 tribute concert)
  - YouTube: Warner Topic channels plus many fan uploads; her Schubert items are scattered across recital compilations. Gretchen am Spinnrade: no recording traced (?).
- **Christa Ludwig** (mezzo, 1928-2021, Germany (career centred on Vienna)) — _canonical_
  Arguably the leading female German Lieder voice of the 1960s-80s; her EMI Schubert recordings with Gerald Moore and Geoffrey Parsons (1957-69) earned 'Great Recordings of the Century' status, and her 1986 DG Winterreise with James Levine was among the first major female accounts of the cycle. Recorded Gretchen am Spinnrade (with Parsons) — direct pilot-song relevance.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: 'Schubert: 15 Lieder' with Geoffrey Parsons, Gerald Moore, Gervase de Peyer (EMI, sessions 1957-1969; GROC reissue 2004) — incl. Gretchen am Spinnrade D 118 and Der Hirt auf dem Felsen; Winterreise with James Levine, piano (DG, rec. Musikverein Brahmssaal Dec 1986, released 1988); 'Les Introuvables de Christa Ludwig' (EMI 4-CD, rec. 1957-69) — further Schubert; Warner box 'The Complete Recitals' (11 CDs)
  - YouTube: Very good: Warner and DG Topic channels (Winterreise complete), plus abundant fan uploads.
- **Janet Baker** (mezzo, b. 1933, England) — _canonical_
  Britain's foremost post-war Lieder mezzo; her 1970 EMI double album of Schubert with Gerald Moore and Geoffrey Parsons (incl. a famous Gretchen am Spinnrade) is a modern classic, and she launched Graham Johnson's complete Hyperion Schubert Edition with Volume 1 (1988). Combines verbal insight with unmatched emotional directness in Schubert.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: 'Schubert Lieder' 2-LP with Gerald Moore & Geoffrey Parsons (EMI, rec. Abbey Road Aug-Dec 1970, issued 1971; CD 1996) — opens with Gretchen am Spinnrade D 118; Lieder Recital: Schubert/Wolf/R. Strauss with Gerald Moore (EMI, 1960s); The Hyperion Schubert Edition Vol. 1 with Graham Johnson (Hyperion CDJ33001, 1988) (?)
  - YouTube: Strong: Warner Topic channels for the EMI sets, Hyperion material partly on official channels, plus many fan uploads.
- **Elly Ameling** (soprano, b. 1933, Netherlands) — _canonical_
  Probably the most extensively recorded female Schubert singer of her generation: a whole series of Philips Schubert recitals with Dalton Baldwin (incl. the 1974 'Songs for Gretchen, Ellen & Suleika'), earlier recordings with Joerg Demus, and Hyperion Schubert Edition Vol. 7 (1990) with Graham Johnson. The model of stylish, immaculate lyric-soprano Schubert.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: Philips Schubert recital series with Dalton Baldwin (late 1960s-1980s) — incl. 'Songs for Gretchen, Ellen & Suleika' (Philips, 1974) with Gretchen am Spinnrade D 118; collected in 'The Philips Recitals' (Eloquence) and Philips Dutch Masters 4-CD Schubert set 464 334-2; Schubert Lieder with Joerg Demus (EMI/Harmonia Mundi, ca. 1967-73) — also includes Gretchen am Spinnrade; The Hyperion Schubert Edition Vol. 7 with Graham Johnson (Hyperion, 1990); Der Hirt auf dem Felsen and sacred Schubert on various Philips discs
  - YouTube: Excellent: Universal (Philips/Eloquence) Topic channels stream the recital series; Hyperion volume also available; plentiful fan uploads.
- **Gundula Janowitz** (soprano, b. 1937, Austria (born Berlin)) — _major_
  Her creamy, instrumental soprano produced one of the most admired Schubert recital sets of the LP era — the DG double album with Irwin Gage (1976-77) — whose Gretchen am Spinnrade is a YouTube favourite. Also a celebrated live Schubert recitalist at Salzburg.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: 'Schubert: Lieder' with Irwin Gage (DG 2530 858 etc., rec. Berlin 1976-77, issued 1977; 2-CD reissue DG 453 082-2, 1997) — incl. Gretchen am Spinnrade D 118; Live Salzburg Festival Liederabende (Orfeo series) (?)
  - YouTube: Good: DG Topic channels have the Gage set complete; her Gretchen has significant fan-upload presence.
- **Edith Mathis** (soprano, b. 1938, Switzerland) — _major_
  Refined Swiss lyric soprano and dedicated Schubertian: a DG Schubert Lieder recital with Karl Engel and her own volume (No. 21) of the Hyperion Schubert Edition with Graham Johnson, plus much Schubert sacred music on DG. A dependable, musicianly rather than starry Schubert presence.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: Schubert: Lieder with Karl Engel (DG, rec. Reitstadl Neumarkt June 1988, issued 1989); The Hyperion Schubert Edition Vol. 21 with Graham Johnson (Hyperion, 1990s); Schubert masses/sacred works under Sawallisch (DG/EMI, 1970s-80s)
  - YouTube: Moderate-good: DG and Hyperion material via Topic/official channels; fewer fan uploads than the bigger names. Gretchen am Spinnrade: no solo recording traced (?).
- **Lucia Popp** (soprano, 1939-1993, Slovakia (Czechoslovakia; career Vienna/Munich)) — _major_
  Radiant lyric soprano whose 1983 EMI Schubert recital with Irwin Gage — including a treasurable Gretchen am Spinnrade — plus live BBC recitals made her one of the best-loved late-20th-century female Schubert singers before her early death.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: 'Schubert Lieder' with Irwin Gage (EMI, rec. 1983, issued 1984) — incl. Gretchen am Spinnrade D 118 and Auf dem Wasser zu singen; BBC Legends: live at St John's Smith Square, 1 March 1982 (Mozart/Schubert with Geoffrey Parsons); Der Hirt auf dem Felsen (various, incl. orchestral/chamber versions) (?)

- **Brigitte Fassbaender** (mezzo, b. 1939, Germany) — _canonical_
  The great modern pioneer of the female Winterreise: her 1988 EMI recording with Aribert Reimann is widely called the first great female account of the cycle, followed by an equally searching Schwanengesang with Reimann on DG. Her dark, confrontational, text-driven Schubert changed expectations of what women could do with the cycles.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: Winterreise with Aribert Reimann, piano (EMI, rec. Abbey Road Oct 1988); Schwanengesang D 957 + 5 Lieder with Aribert Reimann (DG, early 1990s); Schubert Lieder recitals with Erik Werba / Irwin Gage (DG/EMI, 1970s-80s) (?); Gretchen am Spinnrade — listed among notable recordings of the song (Wikipedia); exact album/date to confirm (?)

- **Arleen Auger** (soprano, 1939-1993, USA) — _major_
  Crystalline American lyric soprano, a favourite of Graham Johnson, who gave her Volume 9 of the Hyperion Schubert Edition ('Schubert and the Theatre', with Der Hirt auf dem Felsen); her poised, pure-toned Schubert stands with the finest of the digital era despite her early death.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: The Hyperion Schubert Edition Vol. 9 'Schubert and the Theatre' with Graham Johnson (rec. Oct 1989, issued 1991) — incl. Der Hirt auf dem Felsen D 965; 'Love Songs' with Dalton Baldwin (Delos, 1988) — includes Schubert items (?)

- **Margaret Price** (soprano, 1941-2011, Wales, UK) — _major_
  Sumptuous lyric soprano whose Schubert recital with Wolfgang Sawallisch at the piano was the very first release of the Orfeo label — a distinguished modern Schubert document including Der Hirt auf dem Felsen. A regular Schubert recitalist with Sawallisch and James Lockhart from 1973 onward.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: Schubert: Ausgewaehlte Lieder with Wolfgang Sawallisch, piano, and Hans Schoeneberger, clarinet (Orfeo C 001, recorded ca. 1980, released 1984 as the label's first album) — incl. - **Jessye Norman** (soprano, 1945-2019, USA) — _major_
  Her grand-scaled, opulently voiced Schubert — above all the 1984 Philips recital with Phillip Moll containing a monumental Gretchen am Spinnrade — brought Lieder to huge international audiences; also a celebrated live Schubert recitalist (Hohenems Schubertiade, Salzburg). Outside the born-before-1940 brief but seeded and clearly belongs in the project.

## Женщины: современные поколения

- **Barbara Bonney** (soprano, 1956-, USA (long based in Austria/UK)) — _major_
  One of the most admired lyric-soprano Schubertians of the 1990s-2000s; her bright, pure tone and idiomatic German made her Teldec Schubert recital a modern reference for the 'soprano Schubert' repertoire (Ave Maria, Die Forelle, Mignon songs, Der Hirt auf dem Felsen). A fixture at the Schubertiade Schwarzenberg and in Lieder recitals worldwide.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: Schubert: Lieder, with Geoffrey Parsons, clarinet Sharon Kam in Der Hirt auf dem Felsen (Teldec, rec. April 1994, cat. 4509-90873-2); First Encounter, duet album with Angelika Kirchschlager and Malcolm Martineau, includes some Schubert (Sony, 2004); Gretchen am Spinnrade D 118: YES — on the 1994 Teldec Lieder album
  - YouTube: Strong: Warner/Teldec catalogue on official Topic channels (full 1994 album streamable, including Gretchen); very many fan uploads of Ave Maria, Die Forelle, etc.
- **Anne Sofie von Otter** (mezzo, 1955-, Sweden) — _major_
  The leading Lieder mezzo of her generation; her Schubert combines classical poise with keen textual intelligence. Her live orchestral Schubert album with Abbado won the 2004 Grammy for Best Classical Vocal Performance, and her 1997 DG recital with Bengt Forsberg is a benchmark of imaginative programming.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: Schubert: Lieder, with Bengt Forsberg (DG, rec. 1996, rel. 1997); Schubert: Lieder with Orchestra, live with Chamber Orchestra of Europe/Claudio Abbado, shared with Thomas Quasthoff (DG, 2003; Grammy 2004); Gretchen am Spinnrade D 118: YES — in Max Reger's orchestration on the DG/Abbado 2003 live album; piano-version studio recording not confirmed (?)

- **Renée Fleming** (soprano, 1959-, USA) — _major_

Schubert remained part of her recital programs for decades, though Lieder are a secondary strand of her fame.
  - Ключевые записи Шуберта: Schubert: Lieder ('The Schubert Album'), with Christoph Eschenbach (Decca, rec. Tanglewood June 1996, rel. 1997); Gretchen am Spinnrade D 118: YES — on the 1997 Decca album
